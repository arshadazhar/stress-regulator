Real-World Example

Sarah notices work pressure triggers a freeze response. She procrastinates, which increases pressure, which deepens the freeze. The loop worsens.

She logs her pattern for two weeks. The dashboard shows: Pressure rises → Freeze happens → Problem compounds. She sees it clearly.

In the Intervene tab, she selects Freeze and gets specific strategies: move her body, splash cold water on her face, hum or sing, tell someone what's happening.

She practices one: when she feels freeze building, she takes a 5-minute walk. Small move. Big shift.

Two weeks later, her average stress is down. She's responding instead of freezing. The loop is broken.
